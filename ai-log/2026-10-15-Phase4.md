  Decisions that change §12 (please confirm)

  1. page_view would be Umami's automatic pageview, not a custom event. It
     already records the full URL with all utm_*. A custom event with 5 UTMs +
     variant + entry step would cost 8 events per visit.
  2. ad_variant goes on every event through Umami's data-tag. Tags aren't billed
     as properties, and the value comes from the existing adVariant(), so
     there's one mapping. It also stays as an explicit prop on interaction_start
     and lead_submitted.
  3. Event budget: a fully engaged visit costs about 30 events and a quick
     bounce about 3. Assuming an average of ~10, the free 100K covers roughly
     10,000 visits a month. Umami Pro is $20/month for 1M.
  4. interaction_complete only counts after the visitor actually does something.
     Ad B shows a result on load, and counting that would inflate B's funnel.
  5. section_view also counts a section that fills half the screen. The tool
     section is taller than two phone screens, so it could never be 50 %
     visible.
  6. Playwright runs the real Umami script and records what it would send,
     without sending anything. That checks the real payload (URL with UTMs, tag,
     props). A window.umami stub, as the brief suggested, can't check those, so
     this is a deliberate change.
  7. magnet_opened can't be tied to an ad, because the e-mail link has no
     utm_content and §6a keeps UTMs out of the e-mail. Brevo still has the
     variant on the contact.
